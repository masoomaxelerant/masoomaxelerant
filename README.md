### 🧭 About me

I'm a Software Engineer with **10+ years of hands-on web development** experience, most of it spent deep inside **Drupal** — from Drupal 6 all the way through to today's modern Drupal. I currently build and maintain Drupal solutions at [**Axelerant**](https://www.axelerant.com/), a Diamond Drupal Certified Partner.

I'm a backend-first developer who's just as comfortable wiring up a custom module, a migration, or a third-party API integration as I am untangling a tricky Views query or a content model that's grown sideways. Before Drupal, I spent 5 years at **Aptara** doing XML/DTD content conversion for academic publishers — which is where I learned that the boring stuff (structure, schema, content modeling) is what actually keeps a site sane in year 3.

### 🛠️ What I work on

- 🧱 **Custom Drupal module development** — custom blocks, plugins, services, forms, hooks, install/update hooks.
- 🔌 **Integrations** — Stripe payments &amp; subscriptions, social login (Facebook, etc.), REST/JSON:API, Apache Solr, and other third-party APIs.
- 🔄 **Drupal migrations** — moving content and structure across Drupal 6 → 7 → 8/9/10, and from non-Drupal sources.
- ⚛️ **Headless / decoupled** — Drupal as a backend with React on the frontend, JSON:API / web services, content delivery for SPAs.
- 🎨 **Media &amp; rendering** — HTML5 Canvas with fabric.js, ImageMagick, FFMPEG, dynamic media pipelines.
- 🛒 **Drupal Commerce** — product, cart, and subscription flows.
